With Romit Barat, Ghazal Jain, Dhruv Pawashe and Deepak Jain

A private morning for families who plan in decades, not quarters.

The Wealth Conclave is UpperCrust Wealth’s half-day gathering for a small circle of investors and their families. Four speakers take one question each: how wealth multiplies, where gold belongs in the next decade, how we invest, and how to pass it all on without friction.

After the last session, lunch is served in Pacific Hall. Time to talk with the speakers, with our team, and with people who think about money the way you do.
